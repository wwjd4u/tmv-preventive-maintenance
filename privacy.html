Privacy Policy

CUDD Energy Services — TMV Preventive Maintenance SMS Program
Effective September 4, 2026

This policy describes information used by the CUDD Energy Services TMV Preventive Maintenance SMS program for field technicians and contracted service personnel.

Information collected and its use

We collect your name and mobile number to identify you and route work-related messages. When you submit SMS preferences, we record your choice, submission time, consent disclosure and version, and the page where it was submitted. We use these records to manage consent and prevent sending when you have not agreed or have withdrawn agreement.

Work-order records and messages may contain your assigned TMV, district, scheduled date, selected maintenance functions, and work-order links. These are used for maintenance dispatch, work-order assignments, and completion requests. Messaging providers process the phone number, message, and delivery information to deliver SMS.

Mobile information and consent are not sold or shared for marketing

We do not sell or rent mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or messaging consent. We do not share this information with third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS consent applies only to this CUDD Energy Services TMV program and is not transferred to another sender.

Service providers such as Twilio and mobile carriers process information needed to deliver and manage the program on our behalf. This operational processing does not authorize them to use your mobile information or consent for their own marketing. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

How you choose SMS

SMS enrollment is optional. On our SMS Preferences page, enter your name and mobile number, read the disclosure, check the initially unchecked SMS consent box, and click “Subscribe to SMS Alerts.” Your details must match the technician roster. Being added to the roster or assigned a work order does not enroll you. Consent is not a condition of purchase, employment, or use of the maintenance app.

Frequency, rates, and withdrawal

Message frequency varies with assigned work. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel SMS alerts or HELP for assistance. You can also withdraw consent on the SMS Preferences page or contact jguynes@rpc.net. A STOP reply may receive one final confirmation; no further program alerts will be sent unless you re-enroll.

Retention and protection

Consent and withdrawal records are retained to document your choices, prevent unwanted messages, and address operational or legal needs. We do not automatically erase these records when an assignment ends. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion; some records may need to be retained to honor opt-outs or meet legal requirements. We use HTTPS for the website and limit authorized use of program records to operating and supporting the service.

Questions and changes

Contact jguynes@rpc.net for privacy questions. Updates will appear here with a revised effective date. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.
