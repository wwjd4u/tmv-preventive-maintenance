CUDD Energy Services — TMV Preventive Maintenance SMS Program
Effective September 4, 2026

Program and voluntary enrollment

The CUDD Energy Services TMV Preventive Maintenance SMS program sends recurring operational messages about maintenance dispatch, work-order assignments, and completion requests to participating technicians. It is not a marketing program.

To enroll, use the SMS Preferences page, enter your name and mobile number, actively select the separate SMS consent checkbox, and submit “Subscribe to SMS Alerts.” The checkbox is not preselected. You must be the authorized user of the mobile number, and your details must match the technician roster. Enrollment is optional and is not a condition of purchase, employment, or use of the maintenance app. An assignment alone does not authorize texts.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with assigned work. Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier plan determines any charges. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Canceling SMS and getting help

Reply STOP to cancel SMS alerts. You may receive one final opt-out confirmation. You may also withdraw consent on the SMS Preferences page. Reply HELP for assistance or email jguynes@rpc.net.

If you previously replied STOP and want to re-enroll, contact support for the re-enrollment steps. This program uses the website for initial enrollment; we do not advertise a text keyword for initial sign-up.

Work-order access and delivery

Messages may contain links to your work orders at tmvapp.local-journal.com. SMS supplements the maintenance app; continue to use your normal work-order procedures if delivery is delayed or unavailable. Submitting consent does not immediately send a text. Alerts begin only after program approval and activation.

Privacy and contact

See the Privacy Policy for data use, retention, and the prohibition on sharing mobile information or SMS consent for third-party or affiliate marketing. For support, contact jguynes@rpc.net.
